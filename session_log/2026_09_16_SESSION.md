## 3. Hypotheses & Explorations
- **Hypothesis 1**: Subtractive trace trenches in OpenSCAD require cutting from `z = base_thickness - trace_height` with height `trace_height + 0.1` into the base plate `cube([width, depth, base_thickness])` inside `difference()`. This guarantees clean manifold STL boundaries without coplanar surface z-fighting. *(CONFIRMED: Verified with OpenSCAD compilation producing valid, non-empty manifold STL)*.
- **Hypothesis 2**: Extruding the 2D union of hull segments per trace is cleaner and faster in OpenSCAD CGAL than extruding each segment individually before 3D union. *(CONFIRMED: Passed in pytest)*.
- **Hypothesis 3**: In `samples.py`, traces previously had an artificial 0.85mm clearance gap from hole centers to prevent raised traces from covering holes in the old additive pipeline. Now that traces are trenches, extending traces directly to hole centers allows the trench channels to merge seamlessly into the cylindrical hole voids. *(CONFIRMED: Verified all 7 traces terminate at hole centers and compile cleanly into STL)*.
