- **18:10 CEST**:
  - Investigated and resolved the copper material partitioning bug on the bottom layer:
    - Root cause: In `buildPartitionedMesh()`, `topCutoff` was calculated as `rotSize.y - 0.02`. For layers with corner alignment pegs (e.g. Base and Middle layers), the total vertical bounding box height `rotSize.y` included the peg protrusion ($1.6\,\text{mm} + 1.2\,\text{mm} = 2.8\,\text{mm}$). Because the substrate board top plane was at $Y = 1.6\,\text{mm}$, all upward-facing triangles of the board top surface satisfied $Y < 2.78\,\text{mm}$ and $Y > 0.02\,\text{mm}$, erroneously classifying the entire board top as a trench floor and painting it with copper.

Updated `/api/generate-stack` in `src/pcb3d/web.py` to return `trace_depth`, and updated both `loadSTL()` and `loadMultiLayerStack()` to pass exact `baseThickness` and `traceDepth`.
