Exporting DXF from KiCad

To export compatible DXF files in KiCad PCB Editor:

  1. Open your board in KiCad PCB Editor.
  2. Go to File → Export → DXF...
  3. Under Layers to export, check:
    • Edge.Cuts (Board outline)
    • F.Cu (Front copper traces)
    • NPTH / Drill (Mounting & through holes)
  4. Ensure DXF Format is set to AutoCAD 2010 or later.
  5. Ensure Drill / Pad representation has outline or center holes enabled.
  6. Click Export and drag the generated .dxf directly into PCB3D!
